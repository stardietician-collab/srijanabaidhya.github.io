Know your numbers before we plan your nutrition.

Begin with the Asian BMI assessment. Then continue to the detailed nutritional assessment so your consultation starts with useful information.

Step 1

Asian BMI Calculator

Enter your current height and weight. This calculator uses Asian BMI action points: underweight <18.5, healthy range 18.5–22.9, increased risk 23.0–24.9, and obesity ≥25.0 kg/m².

BMI is a screening measure, not a diagnosis. Pregnancy, athletes with high muscle mass, oedema and some clinical conditions require individual assessment.

Step 2

Nutritional Assessment Form

After BMI, continue to a structured assessment covering health history, dietary pattern, lifestyle and your main nutrition goals.

Frequently asked questions

Nutrition counselling in Kathmandu — common questions.

Clear information for people comparing dietitian services, personalised diet plans and clinical nutrition care.

01

What does a clinical dietitian do?

A clinical dietitian assesses dietary intake and health-related nutrition needs and develops individualised nutrition advice that complements medical care.

02

Do you provide personalised diet plans?

Yes. Plans can be tailored to medical conditions, dietary pattern, food preferences, routine, goals and practical household food choices.

03

Can I consult from outside Kathmandu?

Online nutrition counselling may be available for clients elsewhere in Nepal, subject to consultation suitability and availability.

04

Which nutrition concerns can you help with?

Services include therapeutic nutrition, weight management, diabetes and blood-glucose nutrition, hypertension and metabolic health, kidney nutrition, gastrointestinal nutrition and women’s/reproductive health nutrition.
